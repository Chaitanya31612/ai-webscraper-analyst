# Frontend Design Vision: AI Scraper & Benchmarking Tool

## The Mission
I am building a high-performance **Benchmarking Tool** that leverages automated web scraping and dual-stage AI analysis to evaluate companies. The goal is to transform raw web data into surgical business intelligence and "Accept/Reject" decisions for professional analysts and decision-makers.

## Current Objective
Build a **sophisticated and professional UI foundation** including:
1.  **Homepage**: A premium, high-impact landing experience. It must communicate "Intelligence at Scale" with absolute clarity, prioritizing professional trust over technical novelty.
2.  **Auth Suite (Login/Register)**: Clean, secure, and modern entry points that feel seamless and enterprise-ready.
3.  **Global Header**: A comprehensive navigation bar featuring:
    - **Product/Service Links**: About, Pricing, Features.
    - **Company Dropdowns**: Team, Careers, Contact.
    - **CTAs**: a refined "Log In" action.

## Design Philosophy: "Executive Clarity"
The interface should feel like a premium financial or consulting tool—authoritative, clean, and highly organized. Avoid "hacker" tropes or overly complex developer-centric layouts.

### 1. Aesthetic Direction
- **Tone**: **Modern Sophistication**. A blend of "Premium SaaS" and "High-End Editorial." Think clean white space, purposeful alignment, and a focus on content hierarchy.
- **Theme**: A primary **Light Mode (Clean/Executive)** with a thoughtfully designed **Dark Mode (Deep/Refined)**.
- **Layout**: Centered, balanced compositions. Use soft shadows, subtle borders, and professional depth rather than raw brutalist lines.

### 2. Visual Pillars
- **Typography**:
    - **Headings**: *Satoshi* (Bold, geometric, and trustworthy).
    - **Body**: *General Sans* or *Inter* (High legibility, professional).
    - **Data/Labels**: Refined monospaced accents for IDs or timestamps, used sparingly to maintain professional polish.
- **Color Palette**:
    - **Base**: Crisp off-whites and soft grays for light mode; deep navy or charcoal (#0f172a) for dark mode.
    - **Primary Accent**: A sophisticated **Royal Blue** or **Indigo** (#4f46e5) — representing stability and intelligence.
    - **Status Indicators**: Soft, accessible palettes for "Accepted," "Rejected," or "Under Review."
- **Textures & Effects**: Subtle dot-grid backgrounds (as seen in modern SaaS), soft radial glows, and high-quality "glass-morphism" for interactive cards.

### 3. Key Components & Interactions
- **The Hero Section**: Large, centered typography with a clear value proposition. Supporting subtext that explains the efficiency gains. Dual CTAs (e.g., "Get Started" and "Book a Demo").
- **Product Showcases**: High-fidelity mockups or interactive "previews" of the dashboard, showing structured company profiles and AI-generated insights.
- **Navigation**: A "sticky" top header with smooth dropdown menus and clear visual hierarchy.

### 4. Technical Constraints
- **Tech Stack**: Vue 3 (Composition API), Vite, Tailwind 4, Pinia.
- **Styling**: Prefer Vanilla CSS or Tailwind utility layers for custom components. Avoid generic "Bootstrap" looks.

---
**Guiding Principle**: The tool should look like it belongs in a Fortune 500 boardroom. It must be intuitive for a business analyst while maintaining the power of an AI-driven engine.
